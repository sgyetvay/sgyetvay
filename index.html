CV

I'm a labor economist. Most of my current research is about immigration.

Before joining UQAM, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Ohio State University, where I worked with Kurt Lavetti.

I received my PhD at the University of British Columbia in 2024, advised by Thomas Lemieux and Raffaele Saggio.
